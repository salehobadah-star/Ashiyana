COMPANY OVERVIEW

Who Are Ashiyana Group?

With a defined vision and relentless drive for excellence, Mr. Hasinul Haque Mohammad established Ashiyana in Dubai in 2002. What started as a modest endeavor quickly evolved into a cornerstone of reliability and efficiency, earning the trust of clients and stakeholders alike. Mr. Haque’s unwavering commitment to quality, coupled with a keen understanding of client needs, all while enforcing a ‘hands-on approach’ has been instrumental in enabling Ashiyana to carve a niche for itself. Today, Ashiyana stands tall as a testament to this entrepreneurial spirit and dedication, continuing to progressively shape the Dubai skyline with innovative and sustainable construction solutions. There is no limit to what Ashiyana can achieve.

The company's passion to grow along with the development of the region is setting new benchmarks of quality, trust and competitiveness. We bring success to our clientele continuously by tying to be their partner and enhancing their corporate values. Striving to provide the ‘best-in-class’ services, we are real business partners to our clients. Ashiyana develops innovative strategies; Client based customized solutions; value added services and committed to deliver promises to our valued clients. Our strong belief and vision has earned us a distinguished platform in the construction market. Our professional approach encourages our clients to come to us for solutions.

MILESTONES

Our Construction Journey

Key historical milestones in the evolution of Ashiyana Contracting LLC.

October 2002
Establishment of Ashiyana Contracting LLC in Dubai, launching civil contracting and building works.
2006
Expansion into luxury custom villa construction and specialized private residential projects in Dubai Hills & Jumeirah.
2012
Establishment of Heavy Steel Fabrication & Structural Erection division for industrial warehouses in Al Quoz & JAFZA.
2018
Launch of specialized Ashiyana Landscaping division for external hardscaping, pools, and green spaces.
2024+
Achievement of 89+ Major Projects Delivered including Olaia on Palm Jumeirah, Square X, Oxford 212, and Spinneys HQ.
