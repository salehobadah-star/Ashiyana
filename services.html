Our Execution Methodology

A disciplined 4-stage engineering process guaranteeing safety, quality, and timely project delivery.

1

Technical BOQ & Review

Detailed architectural drawing review, BOQ estimation, value engineering, and authority approvals.

2

Substructure & Piling

Superstructure & MEP

4

Finishing & Handover

Facade glazing, interior fit-outs, authority testing & commissioning, and key handover.
