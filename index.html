WHO ARE ASHIYANA GROUP

Dubai's Leading Dedicated Building Contracting Firm

Established in October 2002, Ashiyana Group (Ashiyana Contracting LLC) has cemented its reputation as a premiere building contractor headquartered in Burlington Tower, Business Bay, Dubai.

As a dedicated construction entity, we specialize exclusively in civil engineering, high-rise structural construction, custom villa building, steel fabrication & erection, external landscaping, and MEP installation. Driven by our motto "Confidence leads to perfection," we execute projects with safety, speed, and unyielding quality.
