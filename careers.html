MEP Engineer (Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing)

Apply For This Role →

Role Responsibilities & Key Requirements:

  • Supervise and inspect site MEP installations (HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Firefighting) compliant with DEWA, SEWA & Dubai Civil Defense codes.
